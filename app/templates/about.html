About JobHunter

A calmer way to look for work.

JobHunter aggregates job listings from many portals into one clean, de-duplicated, searchable index so you can focus on finding the right next step.

The details

JobHunter ships with seeded demo data, search and filtering, employer posting, a structured resume builder, and content-based job matching. The production scraping stack lives under scrapers/; this interface keeps the candidate experience simple while the data layer does the heavy lifting.
